# Modern timeline

The ruler ends at 2026, which is a timeline endpoint rather than a claim that a particular event happened that year. Added 18 sourced stories, and exposed 12 existing 1660–1881 milestones directly on the chart. Event cards are dated point markers; their visual width does not represent duration. Existing civilization ribbons remain duration bands. Qing now ends in 1912 and Ottoman rule in 1922. Sources for new stories are stored in expanded-events.tsv and shown on their story pages. The next illustration phase can replace their text title panels without changing their stable event IDs.
