# Era date ranges

All 24 era labels show start and end dates beside the name. “c.” means approximately.
Dates for archaeological cultures can differ by region and scholarly chronology.
The Stone Age range uses the earliest known tools and an approximate Near Eastern
transition to the Bronze Age; 3300 BCE is not a universal worldwide end date.
Indus Cities refers to the mature urban phase. Classic Maya refers to the Classic
period, not the end of Maya peoples. Han includes the interruption by the Xin dynasty.
Abbasid dates refer to the Baghdad caliphate, not the later Cairo succession.
The Roman bar begins with the empire in 27 BCE, so its label identifies the Roman
Empire with the western end in 476 CE rather than implying that the separate
Western Roman Empire began in 27 BCE. Ottoman and Qing labels give their full end
dates even though the chart stops earlier.
